Temperature & Humidity sensors template represents a generic monitoring solution suitable for multiple applications.
With this template, you get an interactive dashboard for real-time sensor management including user-friendly alarms configuration.

Solution automatically creates a customer and two customer users. 
Those users are pre-configured with read-only dashboard access, allowing you to immediately experience the end-user perspective in full-screen mode.

#### Solution structure

* "Temperature & Humidity" dashboard;
* "Read only" role;  
* Two "Temperature Sensor" devices and their device profile;
* "High Temperature" and "Low Humidity" alarm rules configured in the device profile;
* Customer with two users;
* Edge instance - optionally, add edge computing to your solution.
